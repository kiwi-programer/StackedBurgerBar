We’re a couple of foodies who are always looking for the most delicious food to eat. We have a passion for hospitality and believe that eating out is about the whole experience, not just the quality of food (though the food must be amazing!).

This burger bar has been a long-time dream, now become a reality, and it’s filled with many of our favourite burgers and sides (especially Joel’s famous buffalo wings!). We can’t wait to serve you!

Early on we decided we wanted our food to cater to as many as possible. One of our closest friends is coeliac and it’s always a mission finding a place to eat with decent gluten free options! With some solid vegetarian and vegan options, and particularly making everything on our menu gluten friendly, we believe there really is something here for everyone.

Another passion for us was to support local as much as we could, and you’ll find we’ve done just that. Almost all our providers are local NZ businesses.

Our third goal was to be environmentally sustainable. From Fairtrade organic coffee to eco-friendly packaging, we’ve worked hard to care for the environment as we make you delicious food.

- Joel and Mel
