				<h3 class="mg-md text-center text-lg-center">
					Gluten Friendly
				</h3>
				<p>
					Our best mate is Coeliac, and we always saw how much of a struggle it was for her to find something on the menu she could eat, and how careful she had to be. It didn’t sit right with us, and on top of that, trying to find a good burger place that can easily cater to gluten intolerance is not easy.<br><br>We have ensured all of our products except the buns are Gluten Free, or have no added gluten. And we have sourced an amazing Gluten Free (and Vegan!) bun for those who cannot consume gluten (or are looking for a Vegan option). When these allergies come up in our food truck, we ensure we change gloves, and even toast your GF bun separately, before making your order. Please note these gluten free buns are all individually wrapped, grilled in a gluten free oven and put into a box to ensure its not touching gluten crumbs or mixing with our glutenous buns.<br><br>&nbsp;We add nothing but salt to our meat patties, our batters are made from rice flour, all sauces are gluten free or have no added gluten and we’ve checked every product right down to our pickles (which aren’t always GF due to the type of vinegar used) to make sure you don’t have to worry about eating at Stacked. Please talk to our friendly cashier team if you fall under this category.
				</p>
